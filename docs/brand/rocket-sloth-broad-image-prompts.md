# Rocket Sloth Broad Brand Image Prompts

These images were created with the built-in ImageGen tool.

## Broad business hero

```text
Use case: photorealistic-natural
Asset type: wide homepage hero image for Rocket Sloth Consulting
Primary request: create an authentic editorial photograph that communicates a practical Texas technology partner serving many kinds of businesses, not one industry.
Scene/backdrop: a revitalized small-town Texas main street and adjoining maker-style workspace in warm early-morning light; subtly visible local businesses such as a service truck, cafe storefront, small shop, and professional office, all natural and secondary.
Subject: a friendly consultant and two different local business owners reviewing a website and business results on a laptop at a large wood worktable; one owner in clean workwear and one in smart-casual clothing; natural candid collaboration, no posed handshake.
Style/medium: premium photorealistic commercial editorial photography, believable people, real materials, understated Texas character.
Composition/framing: cinematic 16:9 horizontal composition, people on the right half, generous clean negative space on the left for website headline copy.
Lighting/mood: warm sunrise, confident, welcoming, capable, grounded.
Color palette: warm cream, charcoal, workwear tan, muted forest green, restrained rust accents.
Constraints: industry-inclusive; country-friendly and blue-collar-friendly without cowboy stereotypes; no cattle, no ranch-only imagery, no holograms, no floating UI, no logos, no readable brand names, no watermark, no text.
```
